What
    @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 (npm), the official single-threaded ffmpeg.wasm build:
    dist/esm/ffmpeg-core.js (111,804 bytes, SHA-256 67a48f11645f85439f3fde4f2119042c16b374b910206b7a7a24f342e28dcae3)
    dist/esm/ffmpeg-core.wasm (32,232,419 bytes, SHA-256 9f57947a5bd530d8f00c5b3f2cb2a3492faa7e5d823315342d6a8656d0a6b7b7)
    Unmodified. It is not part of the plugin bundle (dist/): the plugin downloads it on
    first use from the host's model base, at
    <modelBase>/npm/@ffmpeg/core@0.12.10/dist/esm/ffmpeg-core.{js,wasm},
    checks size and SHA-256, and runs it in a dedicated Web Worker.
